# 🤖 Buddy – Your Personal University Lecture Assistant

**Buddy** is a Python-based desktop chatbot designed to help university students manage their lecture schedules, organize subjects, keep track of upcoming classes, and take short mental breaks through friendly conversations and jokes.

Built with Python, Tkinter, and SQLite, Buddy combines a simple conversational interface with an intelligent rule-based response system and persistent schedule management.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📅 1. Lecture Schedule Management
- View lectures scheduled for today and tomorrow.
- Check lectures for any specific date.
- View your current week's and next week's timetable.
- Find your next scheduled lecture.
- Search for lectures using subject names or course codes.

### 📚 2. Subject Management
- Select the subjects you are currently studying.
- Add new subjects and their course details.
- Delete unwanted subjects.
- Store course codes, lecture times, and classroom information.
- Support for optional subjects.

### 🔄 3. Flexible Timetable Updates
- Reschedule individual lectures.
- Change weekly lecture times.
- Move lectures to different dates.
- Detect overlapping lecture sessions.
- Preserve previous timetable entries when making future weekly changes.

### 💬 4. Conversational Chatbot
- Friendly greetings and everyday conversations.
- Understands common lecture-related questions.
- Recognizes spelling mistakes in supported commands.
- Supports alternative names and abbreviations for selected subjects.
- Provides help and usage instructions.

### 😄 5. Entertainment and Well-being
- Provides programming and study-related jokes.
- Offers different jokes with reduced repetition.
- Responds to feelings such as tiredness, boredom, and stress.
- Suggests taking short breaks and relaxing.
